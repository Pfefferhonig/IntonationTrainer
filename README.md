# Choir Intonation Trainer

Short listening and singing exercises that help choir singers hear — and sing — in tune.

**Open the app: https://pfefferhonig.github.io/IntonationTrainer/**

It runs in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

## The exercises

- **Higher / lower** – two notes: is the second one higher, lower or the same?
- **Odd one out** – one note of a chord is out of tune. Which one, and is it too high or too low?
- **Lock it in** – slide a note until it locks into tune with the others.
- **Echo** – hear a note and sing it back.
- **Join the chord** – sing the missing note of an interval or chord.
- **Your stats** – your ranks, progress and a shareable summary.

The exercises adapt to you: they get harder as you improve. By default they use *just intonation*, the pure tuning a choir naturally sings, and you can switch to piano tuning in Settings.

## Good to know

- **Headphones** are recommended, and needed for the singing exercises (otherwise the microphone also hears the app's own notes).
- The first visit walks you through a short setup: your voice part, a sound, and a quick listen.
- **Privacy:** everything stays on your device. Your progress is stored only in your browser, and your singing is analysed right there — nothing is uploaded.
